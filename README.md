## Featured: The Agentic SOC

### The Agentic SOC: Defending With, and Against, Autonomous AI

**SANS Institute webcast | September 8, 2026**

Can an attacker hijack an AI agent's intent and turn its legitimate access against you?

In this webcast, I explore indirect prompt injection, least agency, and the trust boundaries that matter when agents investigate and respond. Two cinematic demos illustrate how an attacker-controlled tool response can steer an agent toward isolating the wrong system, then replay the scenario with evidence checks and enforced limits on authority.
